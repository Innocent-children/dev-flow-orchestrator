## Delivery Milestone Map

Milestones group existing tasks for sequencing and review; they do not create
new completion evidence and do not change a checkbox by themselves. See
`MILESTONES.md` for the complete entry, exit, exclusion, and stop rules.

- **V4-M0 — Safety Contract Preview:** implement only 13.5 and 13.6, run the
  smallest directly relevant validation required by the milestone, independently review
  the result, then stop without a real V4 reservation, reseal, runtime work, or
  activation.
- **V4-M1 — Local Lite Preview:** only after an explicit post-M0 decision,
  complete 13.1 through 13.3 as an inactive developer preview for catalog,
  identity closure, read-only inspection, and V3 fail-closed behavior.
- **V4-M2 — Inactive Runtime Closure:** first complete any of 13.1 through
  13.3 left open because M1 was skipped or incomplete, then complete 5.13,
  5.15, 7.12, 8.8, 13.4, and 13.7 while both V4 profiles remain inactive. The
  required macOS absence-of-host closure is bounded operator intervention with
  scope-blocking `UNRESOLVED`; trusted-host `ABANDONED` and `COMPENSATED`
  success paths are optional and are not claimed by this milestone.
- **V4-RC — Frozen Candidate and External Evidence:** execute 14.1 through
  14.13 in their declared order, under each required external authorization,
  using macOS-only validation. The independent
  `complete-cross-platform-support` change is not a release-order prerequisite
  for this V4 candidate, and neither change silently satisfies the other.

The retired V3 historical non-completions recorded after 12.5 remain facts. No
milestone fabricates them or counts them as V4 work or evidence.

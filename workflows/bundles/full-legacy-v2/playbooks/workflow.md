# Frozen full legacy workflow

## intake
Use the historical all-repository preflight protocol and preserve repository
evidence. Schema-v1 keeps its legacy confirmation UX; schema-v2 uses its exact
preview and intent contracts.

## preflighted
Record the historical baseline-fetch approval and deterministic baseline.

## baselined
Record every baseline index. The final required record advances to `INDEXED`
only under the historical task schema's automatic-action policy.

## indexed
Record current impact evidence, select the route, and do not infer approval.

## impact-review
Bind route approval to the exact current impact artifact.

## route-approved
Plan, approve, and execute the historical controller-owned worktrees.

## workspace-ready
Record all workspace indexes, then use the exact historical planning edge.

## planning
Record and approve the selected direct or OpenSpec plan before implementation.

## implementing
Work only in recorded worktrees. Replan or reassess through a declared edge.

## verifying
Record current tests and indexes, then capture the complete review snapshot.

## reviewing
Record and approve the independent report or use a declared rework edge.

## finalizing
Present the complete handoff and explicitly confirm irreversible completion.

## blocked
Resume only to the recorded origin through historical recovery semantics.

## done
Read-only handoff. Never reopen.

## cancelled
Read-only history. Never reopen.
